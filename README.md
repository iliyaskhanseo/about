## Current Prime Minister of India

As of March 2026, the current Prime Minister of India is **Narendra Modi**. He has been serving as the Prime Minister since 26 May 2014. He is a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS). Before becoming Prime Minister, he served as the Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014.
